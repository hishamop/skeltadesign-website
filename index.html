The Studio

Design that belongs to its place.

Skelta Design is an architecture firm focused on residential work. Each house begins with the site — its climate, its trees, its light — and grows into a home that feels both contemporary and rooted.

Our work moves comfortably between crisp modern volumes, warm tiled tropical roofs and dramatic gable forms, always balancing openness, shade and greenery.
